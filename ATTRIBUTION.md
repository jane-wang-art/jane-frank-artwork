## Where the credit belongs

Display it legibly next to the image, in the accompanying post caption, or in clearly associated artwork credits available to the audience. Make the source clickable where supported; otherwise show the readable URL. Do not rely solely on a profile biography, source-code comment, hidden metadata or an internal document. Agree on print, video, packaging and collection-credit placement in the written license.

For an authorized adaptation, identify the changes and modifier in addition to the original credit:

```text
Adapted from artwork by Jane Wang.
Original copyright: © 2026 jane-wang-art.
Original source: https://github.com/jane-wang-art/jane-frank-artwork
Changes: [description] by [modifier].
```
